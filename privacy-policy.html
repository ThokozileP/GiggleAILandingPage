Who we are

Giggle AI Innovation builds runtime infrastructure for clinical AI systems. Our product, CARC, is the runtime control layer for clinical AI agents — used to govern, review, and reconstruct clinical agent behaviour, and to generate the evidence structures regulated healthcare AI environments require.

We operate under Dutch and EU law. For privacy matters, contact us at info@giggleaiinnovation.com.

Website: giggleaiinnovation.com
Contact: info@giggleaiinnovation.com

What data we collect

We collect data only when you actively provide it or — with your consent — through analytics tools.

Information you provide directly:

  • Your name and email address when you send an enquiry via email
  • Any information you choose to include in your message
  • Booking details if you schedule a call through our Calendly link

Technical data (if analytics consent is given):

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Referring URL and general geographic region
  • Device type and browser

We do not collect sensitive personal data. We do not sell your data to any third party.

How we use your data

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or question
  • Prepare for and follow up on scheduled demo calls
  • Send information you have explicitly requested

We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing. We will never share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

If you have given analytics consent, analytics data may be used to understand how the website is used and to improve its content.

Third-party services

Calendly: If you use our booking link, Calendly processes your name, email address, and calendar data in accordance with Calendly's Privacy Policy. We do not control their data practices.

Email: Enquiries sent via our email address are handled through our email provider. We retain email correspondence for as long as it is relevant to the enquiry or business relationship.

Analytics: If you consent to analytics, we load Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate website usage, page interactions, and conversion events. Clarity is not loaded unless analytics consent is given. See Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Google Fonts: This site uses Google Fonts, which may result in a request to Google's servers when the page loads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

We use cookies and localStorage to remember your preferences (dark/light theme, cookie consent choice) and — with your consent — for analytics and marketing purposes. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

You can manage or withdraw your cookie consent at any time using the link in the footer.

Data retention

We retain email enquiries for as long as they are relevant to an active or potential business relationship, and for a maximum of three years after our last correspondence unless a longer period is required by law.

Analytics data, if collected, is retained according to the analytics provider's settings and our configured account settings.

Your rights under GDPR

As a resident of the EU or EEA, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right of access — You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — You can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — You can ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object — You can object to how we process your data where we rely on legitimate interests.
  • Right to data portability — You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent (e.g. analytics cookies), you can withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@giggleaiinnovation.com.

If you believe we have not handled your data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.
